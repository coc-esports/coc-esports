The regular season is over. Four Monthly Finals have been played, three Golden Tickets are confirmed, and the road to the World Finals now runs through one weekend: the **Last Chance Qualifier**, on **October 10–11, 2026**.

## How it works

The eight highest-ranked teams on the season leaderboard that don't already hold a Golden Ticket meet in an **8-team double-elimination bracket**. Every team can lose once and stay alive. The **top 3** leave with the last three Golden Tickets, and **$80,000** is on the line across the event.

Every match is a 5v5 Friendly War in Esports Mode on Town Hall 18, with a 45-minute battle day. Stars decide the war first, then destruction, then the fastest average attack time.

## Who's in the running

These are the leaderboard positions just below the qualified teams, as published after the September Monthly Final:

- **Win Or Die**: 5th, 134+ points

> The final LCQ field depends on the September Monthly Final winner, who takes a Golden Ticket directly. We'll update this preview once the field is confirmed.

## What's at stake

Three teams go through to join ZOOS Esports, Repotted Gaming, Vatic, the September champion and the China Regional champion at the [World Finals](/worlds), where **$700,000** is on the line.
