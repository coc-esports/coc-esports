Three of the eight seats at the 2026 World Finals are taken. Each went to a Monthly Final champion, and each of those teams also sits at the top of the season leaderboard.

## ZOOS Esports: June champions

ZOOS Esports won the **June Monthly Final** (June 27–28) to take the first Golden Ticket of the season. They lead the leaderboard with **350+ points**.

## Repotted Gaming: July champions

Repotted Gaming claimed the **July Monthly Final** (July 25–26) and the second ticket. They sit second on the leaderboard with **300+ points**.

## Vatic: August champions

Vatic won the **August Monthly Final** (August 29–30), the third ticket. They're third on the leaderboard with **225+ points**.
