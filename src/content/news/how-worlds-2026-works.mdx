The 2026 Clash of Clans World Championship has a **$1,000,000** prize pool, every match is played on **Town Hall 18**, and it ends with **eight teams** fighting for one title. Here's the whole path, start to finish.

## 1. The monthly season (June to September)

Each month follows the same three steps:

1. **Ladder**: teams play wars to earn a place in the qualifier.
2. **Monthly Qualifier**: 128 teams, double elimination, over two days.
3. **Monthly Final**: the top 8 from the qualifier play an 8-team double-elimination final.

The **winner of each Monthly Final** earns a **Golden Ticket** straight to the World Finals. That's four tickets across June, July, August and September. The Monthly Finals share **$220,000** in prize money.

Every result also earns **leaderboard points**, which matter for the next step.

## 2. China Regional Qualifier

China has its own qualification path. The regional champion takes **one Golden Ticket**.

## 3. Last Chance Qualifier

The eight highest-ranked teams on the leaderboard without a ticket play one more double-elimination bracket on **October 10–11**. The **top 3** qualify. The LCQ carries **$80,000**.

## 4. The World Finals

Four monthly champions, the China Regional champion and three LCQ qualifiers make **The Chosen Eight**. They play an **8-team double-elimination bracket** for **$700,000** and the world title. Dates are still to be announced.

## The rules of a war

- 5v5 Friendly Wars in **Esports Mode**, one attack per player.
- 5-minute preparation, then a 45-minute battle in the qualifiers and finals.
- The winner is decided by **stars**, then **destruction %**, then the **fastest average attack time**.
- A fully tied war goes to a **1v1 best-of-1 Friendly Challenge** on the same base.
