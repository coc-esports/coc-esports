Gildra: 7 design directions

The same content in seven visual worlds for the Clash of Clans esports hub. Each sample shows the headline type, body type, main button, a Golden Ticket, a team crest and the colours. Buttons are real: press one to feel the bevel.

Already decided in every direction

  • The Golden Ticket becomes the site's signature object, with a one-time gold foil sweep when a team qualifies.
  • Team logos become crests in the team colour, not flat squares.
  • Gold means only three things: the main action, qualified or winner, and special brand moments.
  • The home page becomes event-aware: during LCQ week the countdown and the ticket race lead.
  • A new Gildra logo replaces the generic shield.
