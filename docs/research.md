## Our current site (what to fix)
Dark navy/purple + gold, Anton/Inter, a placeholder shield as the hero art, TBD tiles, a big-number band, gradient placeholders in news. It matches the critique (25/40): generic template, gold everywhere, empty art.

## The opportunity
Nobody covers Clash of Clans esports with a **premium editorial** site, and the official site is unreliable. Gildra can be the best-looking, fastest, clearest place to follow the scene.

## What we avoid
Cookie walls and trackers · app-like density on the home page · placeholder art and TBD tiles that look empty · gold or neon everywhere · a dozen equal cards · small text · effects that hide the key info (next match, where to watch).
